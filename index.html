        How I work

        Every product ships with a manual. So do I.

        Best used for
        Messy problems with unclear owners: retention, activation, operations workflows, anything with a funnel.
        What I need
        Access to users, access to data, and a clear answer to "what does success look like?"
        What you get
        A one-page PRD, a success metric we agree on before anyone builds, and a working prototype before engineering commits.
        Rituals
        A weekly metrics review, regular user conversations, and written updates instead of status meetings.
        Known limitations
        Will ask "why" five times. Allergic to roadmaps without metrics. Gets unreasonably excited about funnel charts.
        Release notes
        v1 Mechanical engineer: systems thinking, tolerances, root causes v2 Product analyst: SQL, Python, 15+ A/B tests, churn and activation v3 Product manager: discovery to delivery, CSPO, AI prototyping
